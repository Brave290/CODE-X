Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with me

💰 Pricing & Process

How do you determine project pricing?
Pricing is based on project complexity, timeline, and required features. I offer fixed-price packages for standard projects and custom quotes for complex solutions. Contact me for a free consultation!
What's your payment structure?
I typically require 50% deposit to start, with the remaining 50% due upon completion. For larger projects, I offer milestone-based payments.
Do you offer refunds?
I stand by my work and ensure client satisfaction throughout the process. If you're not happy with the progress, we'll work together to make it right.

⏱️ Timeline & Process

How long does a typical project take?
Simple websites: 1-2 weeks. E-commerce sites: 2-4 weeks. Complex web apps: 4-8 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our consultation.
What's your development process?
1. Discovery & Planning → 2. Design Phase → 3. Development → 4. Testing → 5. Launch → 6. Post-launch Support

🛠️ Technical

What technologies do you use?
I specialize in modern technologies: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Python, MongoDB, and various CMS platforms.
Will my website be mobile-friendly?
Absolutely! All websites I build are fully responsive and optimized for all devices including mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.
Do you provide hosting?
I don't provide hosting directly, but I can recommend reliable hosting providers and help you set everything up.

🔧 Maintenance & Support

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
Yes! I offer monthly maintenance packages that include updates, security patches, backups, and priority support.
What if something breaks after launch?
I provide 30 days of free bug fixes after launch. For ongoing issues, I offer maintenance plans to keep your site running smoothly.
